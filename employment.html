Employment & Legal Rights (PWDs)

Overview

Ang Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) sa Pilipinas ay protektado ng batas para masigurong may pantay na oportunidad sa trabaho, maayos na trato, at walang diskriminasyon sa workplace.

Key Employment Rights of PWDs

Equal Job Opportunity

May karapatan ang PWD na mag-apply, ma-consider, at ma-hire sa trabahong kwalipikado sila. Hindi puwedeng tanggihan ng employer ang aplikante dahil lang sa kapansanan.

Law: RA 7277, RA 10524

No Discrimination at Work

  • Hindi pag-hire
  • Pagbaba ng sahod
  • Pagharang sa promotion
  • Pagtanggal sa trabaho
  • Harassment o pambabastos

Law: RA 7277, RA 9442, RA 10754

Reasonable Accommodation

Dapat magbigay ang employer ng reasonable accommodation para makapagtrabaho nang maayos ang PWD.

  • Flexible o modified work schedule
  • Assistive devices
  • Accessible workspace
  • Sign language interpreter
  • Adjusted tasks ayon sa kakayahan

Law: RA 7277, BP 344

Accessible Workplace

  • Rampa at handrails
  • Accessible na banyo
  • Ligtas at maluwag na daanan
  • Maayos na ilaw

Law: BP 344, RA 7277

Government Employment Quota

At least 1% ng government positions ay naka-reserve para sa qualified PWDs.

Law: RA 10524

Tax Incentives for Employers

  • Additional tax deductions
  • Deductions para sa accessibility improvements

Law: RA 10754

Employee Rights

Equal Salary & Benefits

  • Pantay na sahod
  • SSS
  • PhilHealth
  • Pag-IBIG
  • Leave benefits

Law: RA 7277

Respect & Privacy

  • Bawal ang bullying at harassment
  • Hindi required ang disability disclosure kung hindi kailangan
  • Protektado ang medical at personal information

Law: RA 7277, RA 9442

Rights During Job Application

  • Accessible job postings
  • Fair at non-discriminatory interview
  • Reasonable accommodation during interview
  • Optional disability disclosure

Law: RA 7277, RA 10524

Employer Responsibilities

  • Magbigay ng equal opportunities
  • Walang discrimination sa hiring at promotion
  • Mag-offer ng reasonable accommodation
  • Panatilihing accessible ang workplace
  • Pantay na sahod at benepisyo
  • Protektahan ang privacy ng empleyado

PWD Success Stories

  • Deaf IT employee na na-promote dahil may sign language interpreter
  • Blind massage therapist na trained ng TESDA
  • Wheelchair user na na-hire sa LGU gamit ang 1% quota

How to Report Violations

  • DOLE – workplace discrimination
  • CHR – human rights violations
  • NCDA – PWD concerns
  • LGU PDAO – local assistance

Steps to Report:

  1. I-document ang nangyari
  2. Mag-collect ng ebidensya
  3. Mag-file ng complaint sa tamang ahensya

Other Rights You Should Know

Education

  • Interpreter para sa Deaf students
  • SPED support
  • Modified lessons o materials

Healthcare

  • Affordable medical services
  • Rehabilitation programs
  • PhilHealth coverage

Discounts & Privileges

  • 5% discount sa basic necessities

Government Assistance

  • Financial assistance
