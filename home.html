Welcome to PWD Care Hub PH

PWD Care Hub PH is a safe and accessible online space created especially for Persons with Disabilities (PWDs), their families, and caregivers across the Philippines.

We built this website to make important information easier to find, easier to understand, and easier to access. Many PWDs face challenges when looking for guidance on IDs, benefits, assistance programs, and services. This platform was created to help reduce confusion, save time, and provide clear support in one place.

Our goal is to empower the PWD community by sharing reliable information, practical guides, and helpful resources that support daily living, independence, and inclusion. We believe that everyone deserves equal access to information and services—without barriers.
