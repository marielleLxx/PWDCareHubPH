Welcome to Mandaluyong PWD Care Hub PH

PWD Care Hub PH is an accessible and inclusive information platform created to support Persons with Disabilities (PWDs) living in Mandaluyong City.
This website was developed to help PWD residents, their families, and caregivers easily find accurate, updated, and easy-to-understand information about government services, benefits, discounts, and local assistance programs.
Our mission is to promote awareness, inclusion, and empowerment by ensuring that PWD residents of Mandaluyong can clearly understand their rights and make full use of the benefits provided to them under national laws and city policies.
This platform also supports:
· Step-by-step guides for PWD ID application and renewal
· Explanations of benefits and discounts
· Updates on Mandaluyong City PWD programs and services
· Access to healthcare, social protection, and livelihood information
· Community resources and referral support
We believe that every PWD deserves equal access to information, services, and opportunities. Through this website, we aim to strengthen the connection between the Mandaluyong City Government and the PWD community, and to create a more informed, supported, and inclusive city for all.
PWD Care Hub PH is here to guide you, support you, and stand with you.

Tip: If you are not sure where to start, visit the PWD ID Application Guide page first. It contains the basic process that most persons with disabilities and their families usually need.
