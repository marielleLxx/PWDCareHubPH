Benefits and Discounts

PWD Benefits and Discounts

Short overview ng common benefits na pwedeng i avail ng PWD, base sa national laws and local policies.


Under Republic Act 10754 (amending Magna Carta for Persons with Disability (RA 7277)), PWDs get the following privileges:

✅ Core Benefits & Discounts for PWDs

  • 20% discount + VAT exemption on these goods and services: For more details
    • Hotels, lodging, inns, motels – accommodation fees
    • Restaurants, eateries, food & drinks (for personal consumption)
    • Medicines, medical supplies and assistive devices (with prescription/doctor requirement)
    • Medical and dental services (doctor’s fees, clinic/hospital services, lab tests, diagnostics)
    • Public land transport fares — jeepneys, buses, taxis, trains, ride-hailing, etc.
    • Domestic air and sea travel fares
    • Admission fees — theaters, cinemas, concerts, cultural / amusement venues
    • Funeral and burial services (in case of death of a PWD)

Reminders

  • Laging magdala ng PWD ID at supporting valid ID
  • Discounts ay personal sa PWD at hindi transferable
  • Ask sa establishment kung ano ang exact coverage nila
