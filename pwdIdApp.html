FOR MANDALUYONG CITY RESIDENTS

PWD ID Application Guide

Para sa mga Mandaluyong City residents na gustong mag-apply o mag-update ng Persons with Disabilities ID. Naka-outline dito ang purpose ng PWD ID, requirements, step-by-step process, at important reminders.

What is the PWD ID?

The PWD Identification Card is an official government-issued ID card that helps persons with disabilities access benefits, discounts, and government support. It is for Mandaluyong residents who have visible or non-apparent disabilities.

Who Can Apply?

Any Mandaluyong resident with a disability.

  • Physical disability
  • Visual impairment
  • Hearing impairment
  • Intellectual disability
  • Mental or psychosocial disability
  • Learning disability
  • Non-apparent or hidden disability

Where to Apply

Persons with Disabilities Affairs Division (PDAD)
Mandaluyong City Government Complex, Maysilo Circle
Located at the back of the Executive Building

Contact Details
Phone: (02) 8532-5001 loc 596
Email: dpad@mandaluyong.gov.ph

Requirements

Prepare the following items before going to the PDAD office.

  • Medical certificate or clinical abstract na nagsasaad ng disability
  • Three (3) pieces of 2×2 ID pictures
  • Two (2) pieces of 1×1 ID pictures
  • Blood type result
  • Voter’s ID or Voter’s Registration
Note:
  • If the applicant is a minor. Parent’s or guardian’s voter documents are accepted
  • If you do not have ID pictures or blood typing. The City Government can assist you for free

How to Apply (Step by Step)


1

Submit your requirements

Staff will evaluate and guide you if something is missing.

2

Fill out the PWD Application Form

A staff member will help if you need assistance with writing.

3

Processing

The office will enter your details. prepare your ID. and create your booklets.

If you have no blood type result or need help with voter registration.
PDAD will assist you.

4

Claim your PWD ID and booklets

You will receive your:

  • PWD ID
  • Medicines booklet
  • Prime-commodities booklet

Staff will guide you on how to use them.

Fees and Processing Time

  • The service is free
  • Processing time usually takes around 30 minutes if your requirements are complete

After You Receive Your PWD ID

  • Keep your ID and booklets with you
  • Present them when using your benefits and discounts
  • Use your card for transportation, medicines, and essential goods

Need Help or Have Questions?

You may call or email the PDAD office. or visit the City Government Complex for direct assistance.

Important Reminders

  • The Mandaluyong City government has released a new version of the PWD ID with a QR code. Existing PWDs are encouraged to replace their old IDs.
  • The city also has an online PWD ID verification system on the Mandaluyong City website.
